# Geist brand policy

“Geist” and associated logos identify the Geist project. This policy is separate
from the MIT License.

You may truthfully describe an unmodified distribution as “Geist Pet Creator”
and describe other projects as “compatible with Geist” or “Geist-compatible.”
Keep attribution and link to the original repository.

Forks and modified distributions must not imply that Heygeist reviewed,
endorsed, sponsors, or officially maintains them. If a fork changes behavior,
give the distribution a distinct primary name; “Geist-compatible” may remain a
descriptive subtitle.

Do not use Geist logos as your product, organization, package, or social account
identity without separate written permission. Nothing here limits nominative or
other uses allowed by applicable law.
